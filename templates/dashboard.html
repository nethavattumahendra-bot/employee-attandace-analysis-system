Mathematical Attendance & Absenteeism Standards

Attendance Formula

Attendance % = [ Present / (Present + Absent) ] × 100

Calculates proportion of scheduled working days attended.

Absenteeism Formula

Absenteeism % = [ Absent / (Present + Absent) ] × 100

Calculates rate of unexcused absences.

Denominator Exclusions

Approved Leave ({{ summary.total_leave }} logged) and Public Holidays ({{ summary.total_holiday }} logged) are strictly excluded from the percentage denominator.

Zero-Denominator Guard

When an employee has 0 present and 0 absent records (e.g. all days on leave/holiday), the system safely returns 0.0%.
